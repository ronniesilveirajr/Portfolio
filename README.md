## Portfolio Documents

### Canonical Cross-System Architectural Metamodel

**Professional Proof of Work, Transferable Capabilities, and Validation Roadmap**

A reusable architecture-and-governance metamodel for designing heterogeneous intelligent systems while preserving system-specific mechanisms. The methodology universalizes structural locations and questions, leaving each instantiated system free to define its own components, terminology, and values.

**What it covers:**
- A canonical schema of 21 ordered structural positions organized into six architectural domains
- A governing principle that permits cross-system reuse without collapsing distinct systems into one architecture
- Governance, decision architecture, formalization, and evidence frameworks
- A synthetic worked example and repeatable mapping record
- An eight-level evidence maturity framework and designed validation program

**Read it:** [Canonical Cross-System Architectural Metamodel](canonical-metamodel-report.pdf)


### Conversational Reliability in Large Language Models

**A Human-Centered Evaluation Method and Two Documented Cases**

A method for detecting conversational failures in large language models — scope drift, unsupported attribution, unnecessary qualification, and corrections that do not hold. Applied across two frontier models from different vendors, in ordinary task conditions, without advance notice.

**What it covers:**

- A nine-stage evaluation method that uses natural-language interaction as a diagnostic instrument
- A nine-category failure taxonomy with definitions, examples, and detection procedures
- Two documented case studies across frontier models from different vendors
- A root-mechanism hypothesis consistent with both samples
- A formal test protocol and scoring rubric that make the taxonomy operational
- An evidence-bounded position: documented and observed, generalization remains to be tested

**Read it:** [Conversational Reliability in Large Language Models](https://ronniesilveirajr.github.io/Portfolio/conversational-reliability-case-study.pdf)


### Universal Core Business Blueprint: A Governance-First Venture Design Methodology

**A reusable architecture for designing AI-native ventures from first principles**

A ten-phase methodology that establishes a universal business architecture before any business-specific decision is made, specializes it for a specific industry, configures it for a specific venture, and advances through defined phases from foundation to launch. Applied to a real venture in a domain outside its original design without modifying the universal core.

**What it covers:**

- A universal architecture of ten divisions, fifty-three departments, an ontology, and non-equivalence laws
- An Industry Specialization Addendum framework for modular industry requirements
- A Configured Business Architecture Integration process for mapping universal to specific
- A ten-phase path from universal foundation through controlled launch
- A Controlled Autonomy Mode specification for bounded machine execution
- An evidence discipline preserved throughout the methodology and its application
- An application path documented through Phase 5 of the ten-phase architecture

**Read it:** [Universal Core Business Blueprint](https://ronniesilveirajr.github.io/Portfolio/universal-core-business-blueprint-report.pdf)
